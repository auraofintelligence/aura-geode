A six-page concept site bringing together the proposed Aura Geode capsule, a sixty-day lifestyle pathway, personal digital twins, cooperative economics and GAJRA Earth governance.

## Public page

[Explore the live Aura Geode concept](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-geode/).

## Site guide

- `index.html` — the umbrella protocol, pillars, rollout and economic model.
- `aura-geode-hbot.html` — two capsule models, a proposed 120-hour pathway and sensing concepts.
- `protocol-60-days.html` — selectable lifestyle-engagement tiers and wider modules.
- `protocol-120-hours.html` — a short placeholder for the in-capsule pathway.
- `sovereign-digital-twin.html` — proposed personal-data architecture and twin relationships.
- `gajra-earth-dao.html` — regenerative economy and fractal-governance concepts.

Open `index.html` in a browser. The site has no build step and uses public CDNs for visual libraries, fonts and placeholder images.

## Status and health boundary

This is speculative product and systems design. It is not a working capsule, registered cooperative, deployed DAO, operating digital twin, approved medical device or validated clinical program. Prices, loan models, rollout phases, sensor claims, privacy architecture and health outcomes are proposals to test, not established facts.

HBOT and other health interventions have risks and regulated uses. Nothing here is medical advice or a personal treatment plan. Real development would require engineering evidence, qualified clinical leadership, current primary research, TGA and other regulatory review, privacy and security testing, informed consent and independent human oversight.
